Model comparison

Every pane is driven by one time control and matched on valid time, so runs on different cycles line up correctly. The layout follows the screen rather than assuming one: on a desktop two panes sit side by side and take the full window height, four go two-up and still fit on one screen. On a phone the panes stack full width in portrait and sit side by side in landscape — rotate to switch. Colour fill is speed, grey streamlines direction, magenta contours MSLP at 1 hPa.
